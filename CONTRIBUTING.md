# Contributing

Issues and pull requests are welcome. Include your Mac model, macOS version, steps to reproduce, and whether the problem occurs after sleep or screen selection. Share only relevant redacted log lines; do not post desktop screenshots containing private information.

Build with `zsh build.sh` and run `zsh scripts/test.sh` on a Metal-capable Mac. Explain manual validation for sensor, permission and wake changes. Keep capture single-frame and local, preserve input handoff, and do not introduce always-on capture or a fixed session timeout.

Keep third-party attribution and include licenses for contributed assets. Do not commit certificates, provisioning profiles, generated application bundles or personal recordings.
